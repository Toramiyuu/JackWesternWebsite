Our Story

Delicious, Hearty & Affordable

Jack Western Cuisine has been serving authentic Western dishes across Penang since 2008. What started as a single stall in Ayer Itam has grown into three beloved branches across the island — including our Tanjung Tokong outlet in Taman Evergreen.

Our open-concept kitchen serves up generous portions of steaks, chicken chops, fish & chips, pasta, and more — all at prices that keep locals and tourists coming back. Every set comes with drinks included.

With spacious seating and a family-friendly atmosphere, our Tanjung Tokong branch is the perfect neighbourhood spot to unwind with great food and good company.
